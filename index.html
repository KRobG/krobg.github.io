Robert Gieselmann

I am an AI Researcher at Amazon in Berlin developing fast, verifiable, and self-improving reasoning systems, primarily leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs). Previously, I completed a PhD in Computer Science at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, supervised by Florian T. Pokorny, and supported by WASP, the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program. I completed several internships, including at Meta and Bosch AI. Before my PhD, I worked as a Research Assistant within machine learning and robotics at the Technical University of Hamburg (TUHH). I received my M.Sc. in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence from the Technical University of Munich (TUM).
